What is Osteoporosis?

Osteoporosis is a systemic skeletal disorder characterized by low bone mass and micro-architectural deterioration of bone tissue. This leads to increased bone fragility and a consequent increase in fracture risk.

Key Facts:

  • Often called a "silent disease" because bone loss occurs without symptoms.
  • Affects millions of adults, predominantly post-menopausal women.
  • Can be managed and treated through early detection and lifestyle intervention.
